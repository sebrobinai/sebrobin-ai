Before we talk tools, three questions.

01

What does an hour cost?

Payroll and overheads, brought down to one hour of your team’s work.

02

What does an hour create?

Your gross margin, brought down to the same hour. The real measure of what your team produces.

03

Where do you want to be?

Your revenue and margin target, in one, two or three years.

The difference between what an hour creates today and what it should create is your Margin Gap. That is what AI should help close, and that is what we measure.
